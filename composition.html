Composition.

During the pandemic Thomas returned to writing music, premiering a new work, Wisp, in his masters recital at the Royal Academy of Music. The following year, Thomas composed Beacon for string quartet. This was performed by the Consone Quartet in the National Centre for Early Music Composition Competition, where he was selected as finalist.
